Smart TV Quick Connect

Sign in on a Smart TV, Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV or Apple TV in seconds, without typing a password with the remote.

  1. Install the TV app

    On Android TV, Google TV or Fire TV, download Fladder or Findroid. On Apple TV, download the official Jellyfin app from the App Store.

  2. Enter the server address

    Open the app and set the server address to:

    https://app.robocrax.com

  3. Choose Quick Connect

    Select Quick Connect on the TV. It shows a temporary 6-digit code.

  4. Approve it on your phone or computer

    Sign in on your phone or in a browser, go to User Profile → Quick Connect, enter the code and select Authorise.
