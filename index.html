    AUTO-01Data and automation

    Automations that keep running when you stop watching.

    If someone on your team copies data between systems by hand, or a Zapier chain breaks every few weeks, that's a job for a small, purpose-built program. I write them in Go, Python and Node.js, and they run on a schedule or the moment something happens.

    Pull data from websites

    Headless-browser scrapers built with Playwright or Puppeteer that handle JavaScript-heavy pages, extract prices, stock levels or listings, and write clean JSON straight into your database.

    Catch and route webhooks

    Relays that receive events from Stripe, Shopify or your CRM, keep a raw log of every payload, and deliver them onward with automatic retries when the other end is down.

    Built to look after themselves

    Each automation retries failed work with backoff, logs what it did, and tells you when something needs a human. Database syncs run on their own and pick up where they left off after an outage, so nothing gets lost or doubled up.

    ATEM-CTLCustom build, in service

    SSMB ATEM: the AV desk on one page

    Everything the AV desk does, the switcher, the logo, the face bubble, the projectors and the live stream, on one page, on any phone, iPad or PC. A browser-based controller for SSMB Auckland's Blackmagic ATEM 1 M/E Constellation 4K, built for the volunteers who run the mandir's services: tap a source and it cuts straight to program.

    Runs on the mandir network, and from anywhere behind a Cloudflare Access login

    On a phone the same page folds into one column: what's on air first, the inputs under it, and the toolbar along the bottom.

    Each old step at the desk, and what replaced it

    • 3 steps at the desk
      Cut with one tap

      Tap the input. It's on air. Red means on air. No preview, no transition: a clean cut.

    • 16 windows to search
      Only what has a picture

      Blank inputs hide themselves, and one comes back as soon as it has a picture.

    • 3 steps, every time
      The logo looks after itself

      Auto logo picks a clear top corner and white or black text, and steps aside when something moves in.

    • By numbers
      Place the speaker on the picture

      Drag the circle on program. With "Keep the face centred" on, it follows the speaker by itself.

    • Without seeing it
      See what the projectors show

      Choose under each screen, or drag an input onto a screen.

    • 4 steps, then undo them
      Hold the picture

      Freeze, then Unfreeze. Program holds still while you change things behind it.

    • 3 steps, under pressure
      When the PC stops

      Posters, Backup, Push to air. The backup picture goes on air in a few seconds, and the poster returns at the next cut.

    • At the desk PC
      A poster for every day

      Drop it in, drag it onto its day. On its day a poster is the Poster input; nothing goes on air by itself.

    • Away from the switcher
      Go live from the toolbar

      Pick the resolution, then GO LIVE. The list comes from the Web Presenter itself.

    • One desk
      From anywhere

      At the mandir or away. Add it to the home screen like an app; on Windows there's an app to pin to the taskbar.

    Under the hood

    A Node.js server in a container on the mandir's Proxmox host. A small Windows app on the capture PC streams the switcher's multiview over WebRTC, and the page crops every input's live tile out of it. Cloudflare Tunnel and Access put it on the internet safely, it installs to a phone's home screen as an app, and a Windows app pins it to the taskbar. It also exposes an HTTP API and a video feed that other tools can use, and plays a 90-second intro film that teaches new volunteers the whole thing.

    Built in Node.js and C#.

    PING-SIDECAROpen source

    Device Ping Heartbeats

    heartbeats-ping-sidecar is a tiny Go container for watching devices you can't install monitoring agents on: IoT gateways, home automation hubs, isolated database servers. It checks them with ICMP or TCP and sends a heartbeat to Healthchecks.io or BetterStack only when every check passes, so silence means something's wrong.

    github.com/robocrax/heartbeats-ping-sidecar

    Docker Compose example

    version: '3.8'
services:
  sidecar:
    image: ghcr.io/robocrax/heartbeats-ping-sidecar:latest
    restart: always
    environment:
      - TARGET_HOST=192.168.1.50
      - HEARTBEAT_URL=https://hc-ping.com/your-uuid-token
      - PING_INTERVAL_SEC=30

    JELLYFINSmart TV

    Smart TV Quick Connect Guide

    Authenticate your Smart TV, Android TV, Google TV, or Apple TV in seconds without typing passwords with your TV remote.

    1. Install the TV App

      On Android TV, Google TV, or Fire TV, download Fladder or Findroid. On Apple TV, download the official Jellyfin app from the App Store.

    2. Enter Server Address

      Launch the app and set the host server URL to:

      https://app.robocrax.com

    3. Select Quick Connect

      Click the Quick Connect button on your TV screen. The app will generate and display a temporary 6-digit code.

    4. Authorize on Mobile or PC

      Log into your phone or browser, navigate to User Profile → Quick Connect, enter the 6-digit code, and click Authorize.
