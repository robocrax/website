Easy TV Setup

Watching on your Smart TV?

No need to type long passwords with an awkward TV remote. Use Quick Connect to log in with a simple 6-digit code from your phone or laptop.

01

Get TV App

Download Fladder for Android TV or official app from your TV's app store.

02

Select Quick Connect

Open app, enter server URL app.robocrax.com and click Quick Connect.

03

Get Your Code

Your TV screen will display a temporary 6-digit code.

04

Authorize Code

From a logged-in device, go to Profile → Quick Connect & enter the code. Done!
